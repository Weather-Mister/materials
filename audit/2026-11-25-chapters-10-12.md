HW2 wording, values and option ordering live in [phase-homework.js](../public/phase-homework.js), with whitespace normalized from the PDF. The original contains no supplied figures. Worked sketch figures in the site appear under solutions. [Exercise metadata](../public/phase-exercises.js) maps official numbers to question scans, diagrams, hidden key scans and discrepancy notes. The [practice bank](../public/phase-questions.js) records lecture PDF page references. Diagrams in new practice are course-aligned schematics. Original exercise scans open full size.

Progress is browser-local. Exercise 8 working from the earlier Chapter 10 bank carries forward by original question number. Replacement practice questions have new IDs. The public site stores no student identity or working on a server.

Local validation runs with `node scripts/validate-course.mjs`. Deployment validates course data and SVG XML, builds the Pages artifact, and waits for the uploaded artifact ID to appear in the workflow-run artifact list before calling deploy-pages.
