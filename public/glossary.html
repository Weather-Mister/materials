About the sources
Sources and coverage

Prepared from the extracted text of all six ME2004 Chapter 10–12 COOL decks, re-read on 27 November 2026, plus the existing 102-question bank and archived four-page Homework 2 PDF. References name the relevant PDF page ranges; they are topic pointers, not quotations. Background explanations and formula rearrangements are study aids.

Some lecture pages contain diagrams with little extractable text. Those diagrams were not independently visually audited for this glossary. Supplementary details follow the existing source-reviewed question bank. A full visual source audit is not claimed. The live HW2 connector read was unavailable; its archived repository copy was used.

    The five invariant reactions taught here are eutectic, peritectic, monotectic, eutectoid and peritectoid. All reaction equations are written for cooling; heating reverses them. Steel and Pb–Sn values follow the course convention; use a question’s supplied numbers when they differ.
